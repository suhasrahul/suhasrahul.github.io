Explainable AI Mobile Photo Editor

In progress

  • Building an offline iOS and Android photo editor whose AI diagnoses each photo, explains its findings, and sets real, editable sliders instead of generating pixels, keeping the app under 25 MB.
  • Implemented statistical image diagnosis: Rec. 709 luminance histograms, clipping percentage, color-cast channel ratios, saturation distribution, and Laplacian energy for sharpness.
  • Rendered all pixel processing in GLSL fragment shaders on the GPU, with live screen-resolution preview and full-resolution export from one parameter set.
  • Generating training labels from MIT-Adobe FiveK by porting the shader to a differentiable PyTorch function and fitting 7 parameters to 5 experts’ edits, producing up to 25,000 labels.
  • Training a MobileNetV3 regression model that predicts a bounded residual over a rule-based baseline, with out-of-distribution confidence; deployed on-device as INT8 TensorFlow Lite.
  • Integrated MediaPipe segmentation for background removal; evaluating with per-parameter MAE, SSIM, and PSNR against baselines plus a blind preference study.
